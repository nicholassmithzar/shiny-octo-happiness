Vacuuming cleans the surface. We clean what's underneath.

Every day, dust, dust mites, allergens, spills and outdoor soil work their way deep into carpet and rug fibres, right where children spend their day on the floor. Regular vacuuming lifts what's on top; the rest stays put.

  • Hot-water extraction pulls soil and allergens out of the fibres, not just off them
  • Stain treatment for the spills and accidents that come with little ones
  • Rugs, carpets, play mats, nap mats and reading-corner seating

How it works

No obligation and no guesswork. You see the results first, then decide.

1

Free 15-minute demo

We deep-clean one of your high-traffic rugs so you can see (and smell) the difference for yourself.

2

We measure & quote

Pricing is per square metre and depends on the type of rug or carpet and its condition. We measure, then send you an exact written quote.

3

Deep restoration clean

The first clean lifts months (or years) of built-up soil, spills and allergens and sets a clean baseline.

4

Maintenance plan

Once the baseline is clean, routine visits take less time, so each one costs less. Choose bi-weekly, weekly or once a term.

A hygiene report for your Health & Safety file

After every visit you receive a dated hygiene report by email, with a photo of the water we extracted from your rugs and carpets.

  • Proof of professional cleaning, ready for your Health & Safety file
  • Supports your partial care registration (Children's Act norms and standards) and municipal environmental health inspections
  • Shows parents and staff the work that happens behind the scenes
📄

What's in each report

Service date · areas treated · soil level · the dirty-water photo · technician observations · anything that needs your attention · recommendations.
