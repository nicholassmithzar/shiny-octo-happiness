Privacy notice

Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) · Last updated: October 2026

Titan Hygiene Solutions ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This notice explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Titan Hygiene Solutions is the responsible party for the personal information described here. Contact us at hello@titanhygienesolutions.co.za or 073 233 7503.

2. What we collect

  • Business contact details of schools, centres and businesses: organisation name, the name of the principal or contact person, business email address, phone number and physical address.
  • Your details if you contact us as a private person (for example about your home or a rental): your name, phone number, email address, suburb and what you'd like cleaned.
  • Correspondence: emails, WhatsApp messages and website enquiries you send us, and our replies.
  • Feedback: your rating of our service and any comments, and whether you gave us permission to quote them.
  • Service records: agreements, visit dates, hygiene reports (including photos of treated areas and extracted water), quotes and invoices.

We do not collect information about children. Photos taken during a service show treated areas and extracted water only, never people.

3. Where it comes from

From you directly (for example through our website form, by email, WhatsApp or phone), from someone who recommended us to you or you to us, from our own visits to businesses in our area, or from publicly available sources such as an organisation's own website and public business listings. If someone referred you to us, we tell you who it was the first time we contact you.

4. Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and give you a quote.
  • To introduce our services once to organisations that may benefit from them, and to ask whether they'd like to hear more.
  • To answer questions, arrange demonstrations, prepare quotes and agreements, schedule visits, send hygiene reports and invoices, and follow up on payments.
  • To ask for feedback on our service and improve it, and to ask our clients whether they'd leave us a review or recommend us to someone.

5. Direct marketing and opting out

If you haven't dealt with us before, we email you only once to ask whether you would like to hear more, in line with section 69 of POPIA. We won't email you again unless you reply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any email or writing to hello@titanhygienesolutions.co.za; we then stop all marketing contact.

6. Who we share it with

We don't sell personal information. We use trusted service providers (operators) to run our business, such as email delivery, delivery of our website form to our inbox (Web3Forms), secure data storage and AI-assisted drafting of correspondence and reports. They process information only on our instructions and under appropriate safeguards. Some of these providers may store or process information outside South Africa, with protections consistent with POPIA.

7. Testimonials

We only show your words on our website or social media if you've given us permission, either with your name and organisation or anonymously, as you choose. You can withdraw that permission at any time and we'll remove them.

8. How long we keep it

Only for as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law (for example, tax records). If you opt out, we keep only the minimum needed to make sure you are not contacted again.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

10. Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact hello@titanhygienesolutions.co.za. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

11. Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
